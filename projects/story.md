# D.B. Cooper - El Hombre Que Desapareció
## 50-second cinematic 3D mystery short — Visual Script

**No narration. No dialogue. No voices.**
Story told exclusively through: animation, cinematography, music, SFX, Spanish text overlays.

---

## Historical Facts (verified)
- Date: November 24, 1971
- Flight: Northwest Orient Airlines Flight 305, Portland → Seattle
- Aircraft: Boeing 727-100 (aft airstair capable)
- Ransom: $200,000 cash (20 packets of $10,000) + 4 parachutes
- Cooper jumped over Pacific Northwest wilderness ~8:13 PM
- Weather: Rain, overcast, 7°C, winds 15-25 knots
- Identity: Unknown. Only unsolved case of air piracy in American aviation history.
- Money found: $5,800 in 1980 on Tena Bar (Columbia River)
- Parachute/Cooper: Never found

---

## Visual Style: "1970s Cinematic Mystery"
- **Palette**: Deep blues (#0B1426), cold teals (#1A3A4A), warm ambers (#C8964A), tobacco browns (#4A3728), vintage oranges (#D47B2A)
- **Interiors**: Warm tungsten (3200K) — amber pools, deep shadows, cigarette smoke volumetric
- **Exteriors**: Cold moonlight (5600K) — blue-black, rim light on aircraft, volumetric fog
- **Film look**: Subtle grain, halation on highlights, vignette, 2.39:1 letterbox feel within 9:16
- **Lighting**: 3-point motivated, practical sources visible (cabin reading lights, instrument panel, runway lights)

---

## Character: D.B. Cooper
- **Build**: Male, 40s, 180cm, athletic but not muscular
- **Wardrobe**: Black wool suit (1970s cut, slight flare), white shirt, black tie, black oxfords
- **Props**: Black leather briefcase (worn), parachute (military surplus NB-6/8)
- **Demeanor**: Calm, controlled, deliberate. Never rushes. Economy of movement.
- **Face**: Often in shadow, profile, or obscured. Mystery preserved.
